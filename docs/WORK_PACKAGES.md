# BENIGN Work Packages

## BENIGN-WP00 — Scope and novelty lock

Produce the authoritative prior-art map and a precise table of:

- already known;
- reused machinery;
- proposed BENIGN contribution;
- explicitly unclaimed territory.

Exit condition: no central claimed novelty is already subsumed by known
parameterization, quotient-landscape, perturbation, or stability theory.

## BENIGN-WP01 — Invariance and impossibility calculus

Establish the transformations that cannot alter intrinsic local critical
topology.

Initial targets include:

- Hessian-inertia preservation under local diffeomorphism at critical points;
- exact distinction between conditioning and topology changes;
- quotient removal of symmetry zero modes under stated regularity assumptions;
- counterexamples preventing overclaim.

Exit condition: a clean impossibility layer that constrains later constructive
claims.

## BENIGN-WP02 — Quantitative perturbation reserve

Define robust quotient/normal landscape margins and derive explicit reserve
consumption under controlled \(C^1/C^2\) perturbations.

Attempt extension from isolated Morse points to Morse-Bott solution manifolds.

Exit condition: a usable local reserve statement with explicit assumptions and
non-vacuous constants.

## BENIGN-WP03 — Compositional benignity

Develop curvature-normalized interface contracts.

The first unit test is restricted to the positive-normal-curvature sector:

\[
\chi=
\|A^{-1/2}CB^{-1/2}\|_2,
\qquad A,B\succ0.
\]

This SPD quantity is not to be reused as though it were already a strict-saddle
certificate.

Required advance:

- nonlinear local formulation;
- persistence/continuation of the coupled critical object;
- an explicit bound on critical-point displacement under coupling;
- normal/quotient geometry evaluated at the displaced critical object;
- a separate signed/inertia-stability treatment for strict-saddle sectors;
- curvature variation control;
- sparse multi-block extension;
- matrix-free estimation with certified approximation-error bounds;
- theorem content beyond ordinary block-Hessian positivity and standard
  implicit-function persistence facts.

A successful theorem must certify a qualitative property of the coupled system,
not merely the Hessian of the uncoupled system at a point that ceases to be
stationary after coupling.

This is the principal standalone-programme survival package.

## BENIGN-WP04 — Dynamical benignity

Treat the discrete optimizer as part of the presentation.

Measure and analyze:

- optimizer-state Jacobian;
- spectral radius;
- finite-horizon gain;
- nonnormal transient growth;
- step-size/integrator stability;
- splitting error where applicable.

Exit condition: demonstrate why landscape certification alone is insufficient.

## BENIGN-WP05 — Falsification bench

Execute the controlled benchmark ladder specified in `experiments/README.md`.

No neural-network scale-up is authorized by this work package.

## BENIGN-WP06 — Transfer gate

Only after WP00–WP05 survive should BENIGN transfer to existing GCL systems.

Initial candidate targets:

- nGPT / normalized state geometry;
- RSSD / Stiefel geometry;
- MODULUS / derived dynamics;
- BoundaryContract / composition;
- CPS / dynamical diagnostics;
- SPINDLE / splitting.

Transfer questions must be quantitative: which certificate component changed,
by how much, and did that change predict observed behavior?
